About Kothalipi

Bangla deserves software that listens. Kothalipi (কথালিপি, “speech-script”) turns your voice into Bangla and English text in any app.

Why it exists

Most voice typing tools are built for English first, and Bangla comes later, with models that make many mistakes. Kothalipi starts from Bangla: Bangla-trained models, a key for each language, and a setup that works on ordinary computers. It is free and open source so that anyone can use it, check it and improve it.

Made by

TA Shanto, a developer from Bangladesh. tashanto.com · GitHub

Built on open work

Kothalipi's speech engine, hotkey handling and text insertion began from the open-source Handy project by CJ Pais (MIT). On-device recognition uses transcribe.cpp and ggml, and the Bangla models are open models from the community. Everything else is Kothalipi's own. See the full credits.

Support the project

Kothalipi is free and has no ads, accounts or tracking. If it saves you time and you would like to help keep it going, you can support its development. Telling a friend, reporting a wrong word or sharing a recording helps just as much.
