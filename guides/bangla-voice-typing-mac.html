How to type Bangla with your voice on a Mac

Dictate Bangla into any Mac app with Kothalipi, free and offline. Setup takes about five minutes, most of it the model download.

Updated 2026-11-23 · 5 min read

1. Install Kothalipi

Open Terminal and run:

curl -fsSL https://kothalipi.tashanto.com/install.sh | sh

Or download the .dmg from the download page and drag Kothalipi to Applications. If macOS says it could not verify the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway (early releases are not notarized yet).

2. Allow Microphone and Accessibility

Kothalipi asks for two permissions. Microphone lets it hear you while you hold the key. Accessibility lets the keys work in every app and lets Kothalipi type where your cursor is. If a switch is off, turn on Kothalipi in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

3. Choose Offline and download the Bangla model

Pick Offline to stay free and private. Setup reads your Mac's memory and chip and marks the best model. For Bangla that is Qwen3-ASR 0.6B Bangla (about 811 MB). Download it; you can continue while it finishes. Also download the English model if you want the English key.

Short on memory (4 GB)? Choose Cloud instead and add a free Gemini key: how to get one.

4. Check your keys

The defaults are Right ⌘ for Bangla and fn for English. macOS can use both for its own features, and setup warns you if it does:

  • Globe key: System Settings → Keyboard → set “Press 🌐 key to” to Do Nothing.
  • Dictation: System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation → change the Shortcut away from “Press Right Command Key Twice”, or turn it off.

You can also pick other keys in Settings → General.

5. Dictate

Click into any text field. Hold Right ⌘, speak Bangla, and let go. The text appears at your cursor. A small pill shows BN while you record.

Tips for better Bangla results

  • Speak in natural phrases and pause briefly between sentences.
  • Use a headset or a close microphone in a quiet room.
  • For long dictation, tap the key once to start and tap again to stop.
  • For sentences that mix Bangla and English, use the Bangla key, and try Cloud mode with Gemini for the best accuracy.

Problems? See troubleshooting or the full macOS guide.
