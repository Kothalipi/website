Get a free Groq Whisper API key

Groq runs OpenAI's whisper-large-v3-turbo on custom LPU hardware with a generous free tier. It transcribes Bangla and English in under a second and makes the perfect free backup alongside Google Gemini.

Updated 2026-11-23 · 2 min read

Step 1: Create your free Groq API key

  1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign in with Google or GitHub (no credit card required).
  2. Click Create API Key, give it a name like Kothalipi, and copy the key (starts with gsk_...).

Tip: In both Kothalipi Desktop and Kothalipi Mobile, you can tap the Get a free key / Groq Key ↗ button in Settings to open this page directly.

Step 2: Add it to Kothalipi Desktop (v0.1.8+)

  1. Open Kothalipi → Settings → General → Listening and select Cloud (your keys).
  2. Paste your gsk_... key under Groq Whisper (Free, Ultra-Fast).
  3. Click Save. Kothalipi verifies the key automatically.
  4. Use the up/down arrows to choose whether Gemini or Groq Whisper runs first — if one hits its free quota, Kothalipi automatically falls back to the other!

Step 3: Add it to Kothalipi Mobile (Android & iOS v0.2.0+)

  1. Open the Kothalipi mobile app and tap ⚙️ Settings.
  2. Paste your key into the Groq Whisper Key field and tap Test to confirm connection.
  3. Set Primary Provider to Auto (automatic fallback between Gemini & Groq) or Groq, then tap Save.

Free tier limits & usage

Groq offers a generous free tier for whisper-large-v3-turbo. You can check your live usage anytime at console.groq.com/settings/limits.
