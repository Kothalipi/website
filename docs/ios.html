iPhone / iOS

Minimalist voice dictation for iPhone with a custom SwiftUI Voice Hub, system keyboard extension, and Action Button integration. Requires iOS 16.0 or newer.

1. Download the IPA

Download the iOS package: Download Kothalipi-v0.1.0.ipa

The IPA is also mirrored on GitHub Releases (v0.1.6) and the Download page.

2. Sideloading onto iPhone

Until Kothalipi is published on the Apple App Store, you can sideload the IPA using standard tools:

  • AltStore / SideStore (Recommended): Free on-device signing using your personal Apple ID. Install AltStore, tap the + button in My Apps, and select Kothalipi-v0.1.0.ipa.
  • TrollStore: For supported iOS versions (iOS 14.0–16.6.1), install directly with permanent signing and no 7-day expiration.
  • Sideloadly: Drag and drop the IPA from your Mac or PC to install onto your connected iPhone.

3. Enable the Voice Keyboard Extension

iOS doesn't permit floating overlays over other apps, so Kothalipi provides an ultra-slick Voice Keyboard:

  1. Open iPhone Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard… and select Kothalipi under Third-Party Keyboards.
  3. Tap Kothalipi in the keyboard list and turn on Allow Full Access.

Why Allow Full Access is required: iOS restricts microphone access and network requests in keyboard extensions unless “Full Access” is granted. Kothalipi never collects, stores, or transmits keystrokes.

4. Action Button & Back Tap (1-Click Trigger)

If you have an iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, 16, or 16 Pro, you can turn the side Action Button into an instant voice dictation trigger:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app on iOS.
  2. Create a shortcut: tap +, add action “Open Kothalipi” or “Dictate with Kothalipi”.
  3. Go to Settings → Action Button, swipe to Shortcut, and pick your Kothalipi shortcut.
  4. Now, just press and hold the Action Button anywhere to speak and copy text!

For older iPhones: Go to Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap and set Double Tap to the Kothalipi shortcut.

5. Live Translation & Gemini AI

In the Kothalipi app, open Settings and paste your free Google Gemini API key (get one free). Enjoy instant Bangla-to-English translation and polished punctuation in real time.
