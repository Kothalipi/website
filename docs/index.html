Getting started

From download to your first Bangla sentence in about five minutes.

📱 Kothalipi Mobile (Wispr Flow Edition)

Looking for smartphone voice typing? Kothalipi is now available for Android and iPhone with Wispr Flow-style floating mic, custom voice keyboard, and real-time Bangla-to-English translation.

Speak in everyday Bangla over WhatsApp, Messenger, or Notes, and have fluent English or polished Bangla typed instantly at your cursor.

Android Guide & APK iOS Guide & IPA Download APK / IPA

1. Install

Download from the download page, or use the terminal:

macOS and Linux
curl -fsSL https://kothalipi.tashanto.com/install.sh | sh
Windows (PowerShell)
irm https://kothalipi.tashanto.com/install.ps1 | iex

Platform guides: macOS · Windows · Linux · Android · iOS.

2. Follow the setup

  1. Permissions. Allow the microphone. On a Mac, also allow Accessibility so Kothalipi can type into other apps. On Android, allow Floating Overlay and Accessibility.
  2. Choose how it listens. Offline is free and private. Cloud uses your own Gemini or ChatGPT key and is the most accurate. Low-memory computers are steered to Cloud. See Cloud & API keys.
  3. Download the voice models. Setup recommends the best Bangla and English model for your computer. In Cloud mode they are your offline backup.
  4. Check your keys. On a Mac, Kothalipi spots macOS settings that steal the keys and opens the right page.
  5. Try it. Hold the Bangla key, speak, let go.

3. Use it anywhere

PlatformTriggerWhat it does
macOSRight ⌘ (Bangla) · fn (English)Types text directly at your cursor in any Mac application.
WindowsRight Alt (Bangla) · Right Ctrl (English)Types text directly at your cursor in any Windows app.
LinuxCtrl+Alt+B · Ctrl+Alt+ETypes through X11 or Wayland helper (wtype / ydotool).
AndroidFloating mic bubble or Voice keyboardDictates over WhatsApp, Messenger, Notes without switching keyboards.
iPhone / iOSKothalipi Keyboard or Action Button1-tap voice dictation with real-time translation and haptics.

Hold the key while you talk, or tap once to start and again to stop. Change keys in Settings → General.
