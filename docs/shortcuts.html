Keys & shortcuts

One key per language. The key decides the language and the model for that recording.

KeymacOSWindowsLinuxWhat it does
BanglaRight ⌘Right AltCtrl+Alt+BBangla in Bangla script, using your Bangla model
EnglishfnRight CtrlCtrl+Alt+EEnglish, using your English model
Transcribe⌥ Option+SpaceCtrl+SpaceCtrl+SpaceYour selected model and language
CancelEscThrows away the current recording

Hold or tap

Hold a key while you speak and let go to type. Or tap it once to start and tap again to stop, handy for long dictation.

Change a key

  1. Open Kothalipi from the menu bar or system tray.
  2. Go to Settings → General.
  3. Click the key you want to change and press the new one. A single modifier such as Right Command works on Mac and Windows.

Mac: macOS can use fn (🌐) and Right ⌘ for its own features. See fixing key conflicts.
