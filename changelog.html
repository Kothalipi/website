Changelog

What changed in each version of Kothalipi.

v0.1.7 · 2026-11-22

Natural Bangla+English code-switching, App-Aware Tone, Vibe Coding, Voice Snippets, Personal Dictionary, Bot-free Meeting Notes, and smarter onboarding.

  • Natural Banglish Code-Switching (On by default): Speak Bangla with English words or technical terms mixed in, and Kothalipi keeps English words in proper English script automatically (toggleable in Settings).
  • App-Aware Tone & Vibe Coding Mode: Automatically adapts tone to the active app — casual in WhatsApp/Messenger/Slack, formal in Mail/Docs, and structured English developer prompts in Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, and Terminal.
  • Voice Snippets (Quick Triggers): Define Bangla or English triggers (e.g., "আমার ইমেইল" or "meeting link") to instantly expand full emails, addresses, or links offline or online.
  • Bangla-English Personal Dictionary: Save custom names, brands, and technical terms once — sent directly to Gemini, OpenAI, and Smart Polish so spellings stay accurate.
  • Bot-free Bangla-English Meeting Notes: Record Zoom, Google Meet, or in-person meetings without any bot joining your call, and get structured summaries, key points, and action items with one-click Markdown copy.
  • Unified Bangla & Translation Settings: Translation, Banglish, App-Aware Tone, Vibe Coding, and Smart Polish now live together in one clean card with live Gemini/offline status.
  • Dynamic Onboarding & Auto-Download: Device-aware recommendations with a Single All-in-One model option and automatic background downloads on Continue.

v0.1.6 · 2026-11-19

Live Bangla to English Voice Translation, mobile apps, and theme switcher.

  • Real-time Bangla to English Voice Translation: Speak naturally in everyday Bangla, and Kothalipi automatically writes out fluent, grammatically polished English using Google Gemini AI.
  • Kothalipi Mobile (Android & iOS): Released Wispr Flow-style floating bubble and voice keyboard extension for instant dictation in any app.
  • Light / Dark Mode: One-click toggle button on navbar with zero-flicker instant theme loading.
  • Direct Voice Typing from Tray: Trigger voice typing directly from system tray menu.
  • Enhanced Contrast: Optimized readability and colors for AI translation cards and button UI.

v0.1.0

The first desktop release.

  • Two keys, two languages. Hold Right ⌘ (Windows: Right Alt) for Bangla and fn (Windows: Right Ctrl) for English. Each key picks its own language and model.
  • Offline or cloud. Offline runs Bangla models on your computer. Cloud uses your Gemini or OpenAI key, with automatic fallback to the offline model.
  • Bangla models built in: Qwen3-ASR 0.6B Bangla and Bengali.AI Whisper Medium, verified by SHA-256 after download.
  • Device-aware setup. Reads your memory and chip and recommends the best models; low-memory computers are steered to Cloud.
  • Guided onboarding with API key checks, model downloads and macOS key-conflict detection.
  • Recording pill shows BN or EN for the active key.
  • Bangla interface for the core screens (the rest falls back to English).
  • Installers for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows (x64 and ARM) and Linux (.deb, .rpm, AppImage). Not code-signed yet.
