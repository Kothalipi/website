Models and accuracy

Kothalipi runs open speech models on your computer, or your own cloud key. Setup picks the best fit for your memory and chip.

Updated 2026-11-23

Bangla (offline)

ModelSizeLicenseAbout
Qwen3-ASR 0.6B Bangla Recommended811 MBApache-2.0Fine-tuned for Bangla on 1,174 hours of speech across nine datasets. Its authors report 14.74% word error rate and 5.34% character error rate on a 10,485-utterance held-out test set.
Bengali.AI Whisper Medium514 MBApache-2.0Bengali.AI's Bangla fine-tune of Whisper. Strong on read speech, slower.

Figures for Qwen3-ASR are from its model card, measured on its authors' test set, not on Kothalipi. Results on your voice, microphone and topic will differ.

Which one fits your computer?

MemoryRecommendation
Under 8 GBCloud mode (Gemini) for the best accuracy. Offline still works with the Bangla model; use Whisper Small for English.
8 to 15 GBOffline with Qwen3-ASR 0.6B Bangla and Parakeet 0.6B.
16 GB or more (or Apple Silicon with 12 GB+)Same models, with plenty of room for your other apps.

A public benchmark is coming

We are building an open Bangla test set (read speech, phone recordings and Bangla mixed with English) to compare every model on the same audio, and will publish the results here. Want to help? Contribute recordings.
