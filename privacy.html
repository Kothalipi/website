Privacy

Short version: Kothalipi doesn't collect anything.

  • No account, no analytics, no servers. Kothalipi has no backend that receives your data.
  • Offline mode turns speech into text on your computer. Audio never leaves it.
  • Cloud mode sends a recording only to the provider whose key you added (Google or OpenAI), under that provider's terms.
  • Keys are stored in the app's settings on your computer.
  • History of your transcripts and recordings is kept locally so you can review them; turn it off or delete it in Settings.
  • Model downloads come from Hugging Face; update checks from GitHub.
  • This website uses no cookies or tracking. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

The code is open: check it yourself.
